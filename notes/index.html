    Cash or click?

    • Our question: which choice keeps more orders, and more revenue?

  7. 07

    Payment and completion: a negative link

    • X = 1 for COD, 0 for prepaid. Y = 1 if the order is completed.
    • Covariance −0.0277, correlation r = −0.19: COD goes with fewer completed orders.

  8. 08

    31% of orders. 56% of cancellations.

    • 20.3% of COD orders cancel.
    • Flip it with Bayes: 55.6% of all cancellations are COD, though COD is only 31.3% of orders.
    Open slide →
  9. 09

    15 orders. How many cancel?

    • Binomial, n = 15, success = cancelled. p = 0.2026 for COD, 0.0737 for prepaid.
    • Expect 3.0 cancellations in 15 COD orders vs 1.1 for prepaid.
    Open slide →
  10. 10

    Do COD orders have higher value?

    • Mean ₹1,725 for COD vs ₹1,672 for prepaid.
    • z = 0.57 < 1.645 → do not reject H₀. Not significantly higher.
    Open slide →
  11. 11

    Does COD really cancel more?

    • 20.3% vs 7.4%.
    • z = 7.28 > 1.645 → reject H₀. COD cancels significantly more.
    Open slide →
  12. 12

    Make prepaid the easy choice

    • Don’t ban COD; that risks losing buyers.
    • Use a small prepaid discount, prepaid as the default, and a small COD fee.
    • A/B test it. If prepaid share rises and conversion holds, revenue rises. Then confirm with real orders.
